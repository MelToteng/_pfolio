Hello, World! A portfolio first

12 November 2026

“Hello, World!” is often the first thing you print when you learn to program. After years of getting by without a portfolio, I finally gave in and built one. Consider this my portfolio’s “Hello, World!”

When I worked locally, people in my network already knew the kind of work I did. They had worked with me, seen me solve problems, or heard about my work from someone they trusted. I rarely needed a portfolio to show what I could do.

Working remotely changed that. I spent most of my time working from home and put less time into keeping in touch with the network I had built. Now that I am looking for my next opportunity, I want to put myself out there and give people who have not worked with me a chance to see what I can do.

So here it is. The first version is plain HTML and CSS, and it is hosted on GitHub Pages. I will use it to share what I am building, what I am learning, and how my projects evolve.

Let's see where it goes.

If you would like to work with me or collaborate on a project or have question on what i have built, I would love to hear from you.
